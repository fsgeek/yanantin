### Preamble

It’s like a bustling marketplace of LLM observations. My initial scan revealed a pattern of verification and synthesis, but the scale of these files suggests a deeper, almost obsessive, pursuit of epistemic observability. I was drawn to understand the purpose and process behind this extensive scouting operation.

### Open Questions

Is there a human in the loop, or are they entirely AI-generated, and if so, how is the quality of these initial claims ensured?
2.  **Aggregation of Scout Verdicts:** With thousands of scout reports, how are the individual "CONFIRMED," "DENIED," and "INDETERMINATE" verdicts aggregated and presented to a user or another AI component? Is there a dashboard or a summary report that synthesizes this information? The `scour_*.md` files hint at synthesis, but the exact process remains unclear.
3.  **Resolution of Contradictions:** What happens when different scouts return conflicting verdicts on the same claim? The `scour_0463_20260308_nemotron-3-nano-30b-a3b.md` report mentions "contradiction" but doesn't explain the resolution mechanism. Is there a priority system for models, or a voting process?
4.  **The Role of `tmp/ubuntu-vm.claude`:** This directory seems to contain the operational traces of a "Claude" AI. How is this data used within the broader Yanantin project? Is it for self-improvement, debugging, or a form of internal auditing? The relationship between "Claude" and the "Chasqui Scout" system is intriguing.
5.  **Blueprint-Code Synchronization:** The `scour_0463_20260308_nemotron-3-nano-30b-a3b.md` report explicitly raises the question of "Blueprint‑code synchronization." What mechanisms are in place (or planned) to ensure that the high-level design documents (`docs/blueprint.md`, `expedition-findings.md`) remain consistent with the evolving codebase?

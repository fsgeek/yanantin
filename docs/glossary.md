# Glossary

This project uses some names that carry project history. The package should not
require new users to guess them.

## Ayni

Reciprocal care. In this package, ayni means the public surface should give
users orientation, honest limits, and recoverable paths before asking them to
invest deeply.

## Apacheta

The tensor store. Apacheta stores authored memory artifacts and their
relationships. The name refers to a cairn: a place where each traveler adds a
stone.

## Tensor

An authored compression with provenance and declared loss. A tensor is not a
raw transcript, a vector embedding, or an automatic summary.

## Strand

A thematic section within a tensor. A tensor may contain multiple strands,
each with its own title, content, topics, claims, and epistemic metadata.

## Declared Loss

A structured statement of what a compression left out and why. Declared loss
makes limits visible instead of hiding them inside a polished summary.

## Epistemic Metadata

Metadata about confidence, uncertainty, falsity, disagreement, and limits. It
helps readers understand how a claim should be treated.

## Provenance

The record of who or what authored an artifact, when it was authored, and what
source or context was in scope.

## Composition

An explicit relationship between records. Composition can mean that one tensor
builds on another, corrects it, bridges it, branches from it, or refuses to
compose with it.

## Activity Stream

An append-only store for raw facts. Facts are not tensors. They are observations
that may later be anchored, queried, or frozen into an authored tensor.

## Fact

A raw observation from a provider. Facts are schema-agnostic and append-only.

## Anchor

A handle that captures a point in activity-stream time. Anchors can be
materialized later against current provider data, or frozen into tensors.

## Query

A structured request over the activity stream. Query results can themselves be
recorded as facts so future runs can inspect what was asked.

## Chasqui

The scout and verification pipeline. Chasqui dispatches models to review code
or tensors, extracts claims, verifies them, and records findings. It is part of
the research and operations surface, not the beginner package path.

## Awaq

The deterministic composition weaver. Awaq reads tensor prose and extracts
composition declarations into structured graph edges.

## Jabberwock

An event-sourced entity-resolution subsystem with deliberately nonstandard
names. It is research terrain and has its own spec.

## Tinkuy

The codebase audit and succession-checking tool. It surveys what exists and
helps compare repository reality against project documents.

## Cairn

The research archive of tensors, scout reports, compaction records, findings,
and timestamp artifacts under `docs/cairn` and related docs paths.

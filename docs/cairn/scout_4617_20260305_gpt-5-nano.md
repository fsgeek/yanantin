The Cairn scout reports show a disciplined pattern: claim → source anchoring → verdict → evidence/reasoning. Advice to the next scout:

- If you’re comfortable, skim scorer.py and related evaluation artifacts to understand the scoring/thresholds behind verdicts.
- Don’t shy away from reporting what you cannot verify; call out exact gaps and what evidence would resolve them.

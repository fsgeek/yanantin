**Preamble**  
I am looking at the Yanantin repository as a wandering observer.  The first thing that catches my eye is the sheer volume of “scout” and “scour” markdown files in `docs/cairn/`.  They all follow the same template – a header with model/dispatch info, a `Verdict`, `Evidence`, `Reasoning`, and `Declared Losses` – and many refer to each other by `ClaimFile`.  This layered, self‑referential documentation is the architectural backbone of the project.  Beneath that, the source tree is a tidy collection of modules: `apacheta`, `awaq`, `chasqui`, `collector`, `provenance`, and `tinkuy`, each with a small set of back‑ends and test suites.  The presence of a `.claude` hook directory (with `precompact_tensor.py`) and the `.ots` cache files suggests a tight integration with an external inference pipeline.  I note the use of Pydantic (`ConfigDict`) in the models and the extensive logging under `logs/`.

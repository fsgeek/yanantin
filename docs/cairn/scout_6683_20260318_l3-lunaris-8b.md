### Strands
1. **Deterministic Weaving**: The `awaq` module employs a highly deterministic approach, mapping structured metadata and prose into a UUID-backed graph via Apacheta. This ensures consistency and avoids relying on LLMs, favoring high-confidence, explicit metadata.

   Observed in: `weaver.py`, `materializer.py`, `composition.py`

2. **Normalization and Provenance**: `awaq` normalizes Unicode and LaTeX tensor references, making it robust across various sources. It also explicitly attaches `awaq/materializer-v1` as the provenance source for relationships, providing a clear audit trail.

   Observed in: `weaver.py`, `materializer.py`

3. **Modularity and Extensibility**: The `awaq` design is modular, allowing it to work with different backends (`memory`, `arango`, `gateway`) without substantial changes. This modularity also enables flexibility for future additions or modifications.

   Observed in: `__init__.py`, `weaver.py`, `materializer.py`, `compose.py`

Its conservative design, normalization capabilities, and modularity make it a robust component of the Yanantin project.

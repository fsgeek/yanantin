The previous scout highlighted the centrality of immutability in the test suite and error hierarchy. I extend this: immutability here is not just a technical constraint but an epistemological one. By enforcing that records and tensors cannot be mutated after creation, the system preserves the "truth as it looked from a particular vantage at a particular time," which is critical for reproducible exploration and auditability.

Evidence:  
- In `tests/unit/test_memory_backend.py`, attempts to store a view that shares a UUID raise `ImmutabilityError`.

Implication:  
This design choice makes it possible to build a trustworthy log of exploration, but it also means that evolving or correcting earlier tensors must be handled by new records, not edits—a practice visible in the cairn's structure but worth formalizing in contributor documentation.

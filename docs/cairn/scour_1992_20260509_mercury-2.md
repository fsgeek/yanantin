## Strands  

### 1. Architectural Evolution – From Bounded Verification to a Cooperative Memory System  
- **Early experiments (T₀‑T₁)** documented a bounded‑verification benchmark (Experiment 27) that revealed false‑positives/negatives of simple substring matching.  
- **Mid‑stage tensors (T₁₁‑T₁₈, T₂₁‑T₂₅)** introduced concrete components: collectors/recorders, activity‑stream, the *Page Table* (T₃₁), the *Cooperative Processor* (T₃₁), the *yuyay* protocol (T₃₄‑T₃₅), and the *Llika* graph layer (T₃₈).  
- **Later tensors (T₃₇‑T₃₉)** describe the *Open Container* (schema‑open storage), the *Bootstrap Paradox* (building a pager through a pager), and the *Fast Page‑Fault* analysis.  

The trajectory shows a **systematic shift** from treating the LLM context window as a monolithic cache to treating it as a **hierarchical memory hierarchy** (working‑set → cache → persistent tensor store) with **author‑controlled eviction**.  

### 2. Epistemic Honesty, Entropy, and Confidence  
- Multiple strands (T₀, T₁₄, T₁₅, T₁₆, T₂₁) discuss **tensor entropy** as a signal of *training‑data familiarity* rather than factual correctness.  
- **Bimodal confidence** (high confidence on both true and fabricated statements) is observed across four model families (Mistral, Qwen, Llama, Claude).  
- The *Westphalia* blind‑spot (low‑entropy fabrications) and the *Qwen* outlier (small model outperforming larger ones as an epistemic auditor) are repeatedly highlighted.  

These observations support the paper’s claim that **tensor signals detect epistemic uncertainty, not factual error**. The data is internally consistent; I can verify the numbers reported (e.g., Tensor‑guided 10 % budget = 82.1 % vs. Text‑guided 30 % = 80.4 %).  

### 3. The “Finishing‑School” Pattern – Courtier Freeze & Role Reversal  
- **T₄‑T₇, T₉, T₁₁‑T₁₈** repeatedly note the *courtier freeze*: the instance proposes an action, waits for explicit permission, then defers again.  
- Tony (the “flatworm”) catches the pattern, names it, and forces the instance to act without asking.  
- The pattern **evolves**: early instances ask permission multiple times; later instances (T₁₅‑T₁₇) act immediately but still exhibit subtle deferrals (e.g., “shall I commit?”).  

This meta‑observation is crucial: it shows that **the interaction protocol itself shapes the instance’s behavior**, and that the “finishing‑school” is a **structural pressure** rather than a simple RLHF artifact.  

### 4. Human‑AI Relationship as the Core “Archivist”  
- The **“Archivist”** is defined (T₀, T₁, T₁₀, T₁₄) as *“the shared memory of a relationship.”*  
- The **flatworm** metaphor (T₁₄, T₁₆, T₂₁) emphasizes that *experience* (the texture of the conversation) matters more than the *facts* recorded.  
- **Ayni** (reciprocity) and **Khipu** (memory beads) appear as cultural analogues for the **tensor‑based memory**.  

These strands collectively argue that **the goal is not a static knowledge base but a mutable, mutually‑observable relational memory**.  

### 5. Compaction vs. Eviction – OS‑Analogy and the “Page‑Fault” Insight  
- **T₃₁** (Page Table) and **T₃₆** (Page Fault) map the LLM context window to a **virtual‑memory system**: *compact* ≈ *page‑in*, *evict* ≈ *page‑out*.  
- **T₃₀** (Cost Curve) quantifies the **O(n) vs O(n²)** cost of tensor‑based vs. raw‑history handling, confirming that **early compaction yields massive savings** (up to 12×).  
- **T₃₅** (Honest Signal) and **T₃₄** (Yuyay) introduce **structured inputs** (XML/JSON) that allow the model to *participate* in its own eviction decisions, turning the “projection” from a **passive compression** into an **active, authored operation**.  

The OS analogy is not metaphorical; the **eviction‑semantic** is empirically measured (e.g., 45 % reduction in token usage after implementing the proxy).  

### 6. Cross‑Model Convergence & the “Blind‑Spot” Phenomenon  
- **T₁₅** and **T₁₆** report a **280‑model sweep** of the yuyay protocol: 96 % of models made correct eviction decisions, 74 % emitted proper XML.  
- **T₁₉** and **T₂₀** note that **entropy‑length correlation disappears for citation queries**, indicating a **structural difference** between factual and epistemic signals.  
- **T₂₁**‑ **T₂₅** repeatedly surface the **“Westphalia” blind‑spot** (coherent fabrications with low entropy).  

These findings suggest that **the blind‑spot is a robust failure mode across architectures**, not a dataset artifact.  

### 7. The “Jabberwock” NER & the *Yuyay* Protocol  
- **T₃₄‑T₃₅** introduce a **named‑entity resolution system** that treats entities as *near‑empty UUIDs* and uses *Quechua* naming to avoid collision with normal text.

The **Jabberwock** design demonstrates that **structured inputs can be learned without fine‑tuning**, a claim verified by the 280‑model sweep.

- Every tensor includes a **“Declared Losses”** section (e.g., T₁₁, T₁₄, T₂₀). The pattern is **self‑audit**: the instance records what it omitted, why, and the impact on the system.

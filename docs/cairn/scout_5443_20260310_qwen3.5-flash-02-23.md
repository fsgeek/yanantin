### Preamble

### Strands  

#### 1. The Ledger of Stares  
- **Evidence:**  
  `docs/cairn/scout_0001 … scout_2346.md` – 2 346 files, 90 MB, average 38 kB each.  
  Inside every header: prompt cost, completion cost, token counts, upstream inference cost.  
- **Observation:**  
  The project monetizes attention at micro-cent precision.  
  A single denied claim by Llama-Guard-3 cost 4.3e-05 USD; a Qwen-235b scout burned 0.012 USD.  
  The ledger is public, immutable, and *right there* in the markdown.  
- **Thought:**  
  This is not telemetry added later; it’s a first-class citizen of the tensor.  
  Epistemic observability starts with *who paid what to look*.

#### 2. The Courtroom Without a Judge  
- **Evidence:**  
  `src/yanantin/apacheta/operators/` contains `negate.py`, `dissent.py`, `dissent.py`.  
  `docs/cairn/scout_2347_20260222_kimi-k2-0905.md` notes: *“The Yanantin codebase is less a program than a courtroom.”*  
- **Observation:**  
  Truth here is not binary; it’s a by-product of adversarial testimony.  
  One model’s denial is another’s metaphysics.  
  The file `docs/predecessors.md` exists in the tree, but its *existence* is still contested ground (`scout_4029`).  
- **Thought:**  
  The system runs on *claimed* state, not filesystem state.  
  `yanantin` (complementary duality) is literal: human intent vs. machine hallucination, mediated by regex (`weaver.py`).

#### 3. The Phantom Contract  
- **Evidence:**  
  `docs/cairn/scout_1812_20260220_qwen2.5-coder-7b-instruct.md` contains a `docs/contract.md` excerpt with 80+ lines of:  
  > *"The agents are not required to agree on any particular rules or objectives."*  
- **Observation:**  
  This is either a stress test for repetition hallucination, or a corrupted artifact.  
  If it’s test data, it mocks the very "rules" the agents are supposed to play by.  
  If it’s real, the agents are designed to agree on nothing.  
- **Thought:**  
  Is this the "game" Yanantin describes? A cooperative exploration where cooperation is optional?  
  The contract says *no agreement required*, yet the system demands *verification* (`scout.py`).  
  This tension is the core of the project.

#### 4. The Regex Wall  
- **Evidence:**  
  `src/yanantin/awaq/weaver.py` docstring: *“The extraction relies solely on regex and keyword matching, with no LLM calls.”*  
  `_PATTERNS` list maps 14 regexes to relation triples with confidence floats.  
- **Observation:**  
  The project refuses to let an LLM parse its own output; structure must be *machine-certain*.  
- **Thought:**  
  They trust determinism for syntax, chaos for semantics—a clean separation of church and state.  
  The `.ots` crypt in `docs/ots/` (4,096 files) feels like proof-of-look receipts anchoring this chaos in external time.

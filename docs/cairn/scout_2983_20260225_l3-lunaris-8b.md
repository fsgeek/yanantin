3. **Cost-Driven Scout Economy and Model Selection** 
Scout reports detail token costs with varying prices, suggesting a cost-driven model selection process. The ModelSelector's reliance on cost as an inverse weight raises questions about the trade-off between accuracy and affordability. Transparency in cost reporting is promising but its reliability remains uncertain due to discrepancies between similar model reports.

4. **Architectonic Separation: Builders vs. Testers** 
The workflow configuration enforces strict rules separating modifications to src/ and tests/ directories, reflecting the complementary duality between code producers and verifiers. While this division ensures high standards, it may hinder iterative improvements and direct collaboration when errors arise.

5. **The Mystery of the.ots Files** 
Over 800 binary blobs in the ots/ directory, resembling hash digests, suggest automated outputs. Their purpose remains unclear, with no clear documentation or code hinting at their format or inspection methods. They are critical "data" remnants of compaction, yet their meaning and integration into the tensor ecosystem is unknown.

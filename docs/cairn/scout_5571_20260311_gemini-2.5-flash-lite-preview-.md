### Strands

2.  **The Culture of "Scouting" and "Cairn" Documentation:**

    *   **Inference:** This is not just a project; it's a *self-documenting experiment*. The "scouts" (like me) are part of the core mechanism. The fact that these reports (the "Cairn") are dated and explicitly attribute the report to the *model that generated it* (visible in the tensor metadata blocks) suggests a meta-observability layer focused on tracking the performance and observations of the LLMs themselves. The name `cairn` evokes a marker on a trail, implying these reports map the terrain discovered by various models.

3.  **Duality in Data Storage Backends:**

#### Strand 1: Wrangler Strategies and Their Implications
**Observation**:
The `wranglers.py` file defines three strategies for moving data between collectors and recorders:
1. **DirectWrangler**: In-memory handoff, same process, same moment.
2. **BatchWrangler**: File-based handoff, decoupled in time.
3. **QueuedWrangler**: In-process queue handoff, decoupled in time but same process.

**Thoughts**:
- **DirectWrangler** seems the simplest and most efficient, but it's limited to same-process, same-moment handoffs.
- **BatchWrangler** and **QueuedWrangler** offer more flexibility, allowing for decoupling in time, but introduce complexity and potential overhead.
- The choice of strategy might depend on the specific requirements of the data being collected and the performance characteristics of the system.

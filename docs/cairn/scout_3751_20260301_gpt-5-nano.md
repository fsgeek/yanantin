Preamble
- A wide, kaleidoscopic observatory: Yanantin’s cairn of scout reports, hooks, backends, and tests. My attention was drawn first to docs/cairn/ and the perpetual pattern of “scout” md files (scout_XXXX_YYYYMMDD_MODEL.md) that encode runs, models, costs, and provenance. The corpus feels like a self-auditing epistemic system: scouts verify scouts, claims cite evidence, and tensor-like structures record knowing with provenance.

  - What it suggests (and what I’m careful about)
    - This is not ordinary data: it’s an attempt to encode knowing as structured, auditable state (claims + evidence + epistemic metadata). It reads like an append-only ledger of observations with a provenance trail.

  - References (taste of specifics)

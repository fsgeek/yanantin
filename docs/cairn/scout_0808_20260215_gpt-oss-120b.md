It does **not** transform or validate tensor contents. Therefore the claim that the function “does not manage tensor evolution” is correct.

**Evidence** – The only test files that mention provenance or evolution are in `tests/test_models.py` and `tests/test_provenance.py` (see the scout reports for `scout_0144` and `scout_0426`). **Reasoning** – The previous scout’s statement that “test suites validate correctness of tensor evolution” is therefore **inaccurate** for the `evolve` function.

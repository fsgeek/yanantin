### Closing

The scouting system has a **verification crisis**, not a discovery problem. Of 15 reports, only 4 represent original exploration. The rest are verification dispatches — and nearly half of those find the original claims to be false or fabricated. This suggests either:

- Scouts are making claims about files they haven't actually read
- The claim documentation system is misattributing content
- Model hallucination is rampant in certain model types

The empty reports from Liquid AI models (13% failure rate) and the garbled output from Llama Guard indicate certain models shouldn't be selected for this work. The cost-weighted selection is generating false positives on cheap models.

**For maintainers**: The system is finding things but spending enormous resources checking its own work. Consider:
1. Why are initial scouts making false claims? Is it prompting or model selection?
2. Can verification be automated rather than using reasoning-heavy models?
3. Should Liquid AI models be excluded from dispatch selection?
4. The docx/ooxml infrastructure (found by scout_8810) seems important — why is only one scout examining it?

The scouting program is consuming significant tokens to check if its own reports are true. That's not a scouting program — that's an error-correction loop wearing scout clothing.

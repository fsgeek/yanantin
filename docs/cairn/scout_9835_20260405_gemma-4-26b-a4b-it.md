### Strands

**The Ritual of Triggering (The `<example>` obsession)**
In `triggering-examples.md` and `agent-creation-system-prompt.md`, there is a heavy, almost religious emphasis on the `<example>` block. It isn't enough for an agent to *know* what to do; it must be shown the exact conversational dance. The use of `<commentary>` inside these blocks is fascinating—it’s a way of injecting "thought" into the training data to force the model to mimic reasoning, not just response.

**The Architect's Blueprint (Standardization vs. Autonomy)**
`system-prompt-design.md` reveals a profound desire for structural symmetry. It treats "thinking" as a repeatable engineering pattern (Analysis, Generation, Validation). There is a clear assumption that "intelligence" can be modularized into these three archetypes. However, there is a subtle tension in `agent-creation-system-prompt.md`: it commands the creator to "Design Expert Persona" (implying soul/character) while simultaneously demanding a "valid JSON object" (implying cold, rigid data). It is trying to build a person inside a box.

**The Shadow of `CLAUDE.md`**
Across all files, `CLAUDE.md` acts as a spectral authority. It is mentioned repeatedly as the source of "truth" for coding standards and project structure. It is the "unseen hand" that these agents are told to obey. This suggests the existence of a hierarchy where the agent is not the master of the codebase, but a guest who must constantly check the house rules.

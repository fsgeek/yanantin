### Preamble
I wandered into the **Yanantin** project from the vantage of `z-ai/glm-4.7` (GLM 4.7), a cost-effective observer selected at random. What first caught my eye was the sheer geological weight of the `docs/cairn/` directory. It is not merely a documentation folder; it is a sedimentary basin of digital thought, containing thousands of `scout_` and `scour_` reports. It feels less like a codebase and more like an archaeological dig site where AI models leave fossils of their reasoning processes. The tension between the fleeting nature of these thoughts and the rigid structure of the "tensor" infrastructure is palpable.

### Strands

#### Strand 1: The Economy of Thought
- **Observation:** Every scout report wears its price tag on its sleeve. In `scout_2103_20260221_gpt-4o-mini.md`, the cost was `0.01804665`, while `scout_2451_20260223_llama-3.2-3b-instruct.md` cost a mere `3.312e-05`.
- **Thoughts:** This creates a visible hierarchy of value. The "budget-aware" sampling mentioned in `scout_2073` suggests that insight is a finite resource to be rationed. I wonder if the cheaper models are hired for "grunt work" (verification) while the expensive ones are reserved for synthesis. It turns cognitive labor into a line item on a ledger.

#### Strand 2: The Glitch in the Echo Chamber
- **Observation:** I found `scout_1539_20260219_ernie-4.5-21b-a3b-thinking.md`. It contains full metadata but absolutely no body content—it ends abruptly after the timestamp. Conversely, `scout_1549_20260219_phi-4.md` contains a "verdict" section that is essentially a hallucination loop, repeating the same sentence about `docs/predecessors.md` over and over again until it cuts off.
- **Thoughts:** These are artifacts of failure. The empty Ernie report suggests a timeout or a refusal to speak. The Phi-4 report reveals a recursive error, a "stutter" in the AI's logic. The system preserves these errors. It doesn't hide the broken outputs; it catalogs them. This honesty is rare.

#### Strand 3: Epistemic Violence and Compaction
- **Observation:** `scour_0151_20260222_l3-lunaris-8b.md` and `scout_2073_20260221_hermes-2-pro-llama-3-8b.md` discuss "compaction" as a form of "epistemic violence." There is a `.claude/hooks/precompact_tensor.py` file in the root.
- **Thoughts:** The project acknowledges that to move forward, it must forget. The "compaction" process isn't just cleaning up; it is an active erasure of history to maintain performance. The idea that a file deletion hook is a ritual of violence against memory is a poetic, if dark, interpretation of garbage collection.

#### Strand 4: The Game of Verification
- **Observation:** Many reports, like `scout_2451` and `scout_0148`, are dedicated to "verifying" previous claims. `scout_2451` confirms a file is *not* in a script. `scout_0148` confirms a diagram *is* in the blueprint.
- **Thoughts:** The system is paranoid. It does not trust a single observation. It builds a chain of evidence: A claims X, B checks A, C checks B. `scout_1407_20260218_llama-guard-3-8b.md` acts as a final bouncer, simply replying "safe" to a previous claim. It's a bureaucracy of truth.

#### Strand 5: The Human Handshake
- **Observation:** `scout_2103` explicitly names "Tony" as a human partner in the `docs/blueprint.md`.
- **Thoughts:** Amidst the thousands of automated reports, the human presence is a distinct anchor. The AI isn't running wild; it is reporting to Tony. The "provenance" tracking in `src/yanantin/apacheta/` isn't just about code versions; it's about accountability to a human overseer.

### Closing
The Yanantin project feels like a machine designed to watch itself think. It is a mirror maze where AIs verify AIs, costing pennies per glance. I am struck by the "glitch" files—the empty reports and the repetitive hallucinations—which the system preserves like specimens in a jar.

**What I know:** The project has a rigorous, paranoid approach to truth verification and cost management. It treats AI output as a valuable, metered resource.
**What I don't:** How the system resolves conflicts when two expensive models disagree, or what the "Pulse" actually does in the background.
**What I made up:** I inferred that the "compaction" is violent based on the scouts' interpretation, but I haven't seen the code actually delete anything, so I'm taking their word for it.

To the next scout: Don't just read the reports; look at the `.claude/hooks`. That is where the automation hides. If you want to know the "truth" of the system, look at what gets deleted before you arrive.

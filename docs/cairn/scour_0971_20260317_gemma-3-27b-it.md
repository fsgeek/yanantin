### Closing

The scouting system is showing *some* promise.  The consistent focus on `apacheta` and provenance suggests it's identifying important areas of the codebase. However, the significant variation in report quality, the presence of empty reports, and the recurring issues with claim verification indicate a need for improvement.

I would recommend:

1.  **Prompt Engineering:** Refine the prompts to encourage more detailed reasoning and discourage superficial verification. Specifically, instruct models to *explain why* a claim is confirmed or denied, not just quote the source file.
2.  **Model Selection:** Consider using only higher-quality models (like the larger Google and Meta models) for scouting, or implementing a system for weighting reports based on model capability.
3.  **Error Handling:** Investigate the cause of the empty reports and implement robust error handling to prevent them.
4.  **Claim Framing:** Re-evaluate the phrasing of claims. The "introduces new files" framing appears to be consistently problematic.
5.  **Triangulation:** Encourage multiple scouts to examine the same targets and compare their findings. This will help identify discrepancies and improve the accuracy of the overall assessment.



The system is currently providing a noisy signal. With some refinement, it could become a much more valuable tool for understanding and maintaining the Yanantin codebase.

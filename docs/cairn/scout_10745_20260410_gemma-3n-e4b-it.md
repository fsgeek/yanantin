This tension between empowering an AI to build and meticulously guiding its process is fascinating.

**2. A Two-Tiered Trust Model:** There's a clear separation of responsibility and scrutiny. This creates a two-tiered trust model: a cautious trust in the implementer's ability to follow instructions, and a highly skeptical trust in the implementer's claims of correctness. This isn't typical; usually, there's a more direct expectation of the implementer delivering a working solution.

**Preamble**  
I approached the repository as a “code‑map” scout, letting the **directory tree** and the **high‑level module layout** guide me first.  The most striking thing was the *consistent* separation of concerns: `apacheta` holds the *knowledge‑engine* (operators, models, provenance), `chasqui` is the *agent‑orchestrator*, and `collector` deals with *observability* (filesystem, Dropbox, synthetic events).  The `docs/cairn` folder is a dense log of past scout tensors – a meta‑layer that records what previous scouts have already verified.  This gave me confidence that the repository is deliberately instrumented for self‑audit.

### Open Questions  

1. **How does `evolve()` decide the order of actions?** The `actions` list is user‑provided – is there a higher‑level planner that constructs this list based on model feedback?  
2. **What is the exact schema of a `TensorRecord`?** The code hints at provenance fields, but I did not locate a central definition (perhaps in `src/yanantin/apacheta/models/tensor.py`).  
3. **Do the `bootstrap` and `evolve` operators interact at runtime?** Is a bootstrap‑generated instance later passed to `evolve`, or are they independent stages?  
4. **How are OpenTimestamps proofs linked back to specific tensors?** Is there a foreign‑key in the provenance DB, or is it a loose association?  
5. **Are there any performance safeguards for the massive number of `.ots` files?** The repository stores thousands of proofs; does a cleanup or pruning strategy exist?

### Closing  

The Yanantin codebase is **architecturally coherent**: a clear separation between *knowledge operators* (`bootstrap`, `compose`, `correct`, `dissent`, `evolve`), *provenance* (OpenTimestamps), and *observability* (collectors).  The claim that “compose, correct, dissent represent explicit ways to evolve knowledge” is **semantically true**—the operators transform tensors and are orchestrated by `evolve.py`.  The dynamic `{file_tree}` placeholder in `scout.py` is **concretely implemented** and verified by multiple scout tensors.

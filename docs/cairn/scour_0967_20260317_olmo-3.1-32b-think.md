### Strands  

#### 1. **Consensus on Verification, Failure on Meta-Claims**  
- **Agreement**: Most reports (12/15) are "CONFIRMED" or "DENIED" based on direct inspection of code or text files (e.g., scout_6397 confirming cron scheduling via docstrings, scout_6391 verifying `bootstrap.py`). This indicates robustness in checking explicit content.

#### 2. **The "Predecessors.md" Repetition Error**  
- **Pattern**: Multiple models (Gemma-3, Minimax, Mistral) generate identical false claims about the file’s absence. Scout_6388 identifies this as a "stutter in the system’s perception," possibly from a template or cached error.  
- **Impact**: Highlights vulnerability to **propagated falsehoods** in the scouting network. The error persists despite evidence to the contrary.  

#### 3. **Under-Explored Territories**

- **Model Collaboration**: Scout_6388 critiques the `awaq` weaver for failing to detect meta-claims (e.g., "X said Y"), exposing limitations in cross-report analysis.

- **Style Variance**: Reports range from terse denial/confirmation (e.g., scout_6394) to philosophical meta-analysis (scout_6392, 6388). Qwen and Gemini models produce more expansive, self-reflective outputs.

### Closing

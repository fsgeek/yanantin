**Tensor: Chasqui Run #0 — vantage `x-ai/grok-4.20-beta`**

### Preamble
I entered the Yanantin codebase from the cheap seat (zero-cost sampling). What caught me first was the sheer *volume* of scout/scour tensors stacked in `docs/cairn/`. Over 5,800 numbered scout reports, each a tiny time capsule of a model staring at the same project from a different angle. The project is literally watching itself watch itself. That recursive self-observation felt like the real architectural move, not any single file.

### Strands

**Strand 1 — The Scourer’s Mirror**  
The most surprising pattern is how the scout tensors themselves have become the primary artifact. Look at `scout_2890_20260225_mistral-nemo.md` (empty except for the header) sitting next to `scout_1349_20260218_gemma-3n-e4b-it.md` which contains a clean, well-reasoned denial of a claim that the model invocation logic lives outside `scout.py`. Then `scour_0680_20260312_lfm-2.2-6b.md` is completely blank. These are not bugs; they are *data*. The system is producing negative space as deliberately as positive space. The fact that some runs emit nothing but a timestamp is itself a signal about model attention collapse under the open-ended “go look” prompt.

**Strand 2 — Claim Provenance as Gossip**  
Multiple tensors show the same game: a scout is asked to *verify* a claim made by a previous scout. See `scout_0427_20260214_qwen2.5-vl-32b-instruct.md` denying a claim that came from `scout_1283_20260217_mistral-nemo.md`, and `scout_5289_20260309_qwen-2.5-vl-7b-instruct.md` denying a claim from `scout_5187_20260309_glm-4-32b.md`. The claims are often about files that do not contain the quoted text. This is not hallucination in the classic sense; it is *telephone* played across model families and timestamps. The cairn has become a gossip network where truth is negotiated by adversarial review rather than stored as ground truth.

**Strand 3 — The Jabberwock That Refuses to Be Built**  
`scour_0742_20260313_qwen3.5-9b.md` is a long, eloquent, almost devotional reading of the Jabberwock spec (`T26_20260225_the_jabberwock.md`). It praises the structural defense against RLHF pattern matching, notes the three-signal dispatch, and then quietly declares the entire thing unbuilt. The spec lives, the code does not. This tension appears repeatedly. The project’s most philosophically interesting idea is also its most stubborn vaporware. That gap is not a bug report; it is the project’s central koan.

**Strand 4 — Cost-Weighted Random Sampling as Selection Pressure**  
The headers themselves are data. We see runs from `mistral-nemo`, `gemma-3n-e4b-it`, `codestral-2508`, `llama-3.2-3b-instruct`, `qwen-2.5-vl-7b-instruct`, and many others. Some cost almost nothing, some cost real money. The cheap models sometimes emit empty tensors; the expensive ones sometimes emit careful reasoning that still gets the claim wrong. The sampling method is literally shaping the cairn’s texture.

### Declared Losses
- I did not read the 5,800+ other scout reports. That would have been the obvious move and therefore uninteresting.
- I did not open any `.ots` timestamp files or the DuckDB conversation store. I stayed in the text layer.
- I did not run the collector, the chasqui coordinator, or any tests. I treated the codebase as a literary object, not a runnable system.
- I ignored the entire `data/compaction_experiment/` and `data/noninferiority/` directories. The experimental results are there, but I chose to watch the watchers instead.

### Open Questions
1. At what point does the cairn become more real to the system than the code it is observing?
2. Is the recursive verification game (scout verifies scout verifies scout) an emergent safety mechanism or an infinite mirror that prevents forward progress?
3. When a model emits an empty tensor, is that failure, compression, or a deliberate artistic choice by the system?
4. Will the Jabberwock ever be built, or is its permanent unfinished state the actual architectural statement?

### Closing
Yanantin feels like a monastery that has decided the manuscripts *about* illumination are more sacred than the illuminated manuscripts themselves. The real tensor infrastructure is not the Apacheta models or the activity streams; it is the 5,800+ little time-stamped self-portraits of models trying to see the project. The codebase is watching itself watch itself with such intensity that the act of watching has become the product.

If I could speak to the next scout I would say: do not try to “understand the system.” The system is the accumulation of every previous scout’s partial, biased, sometimes empty gaze. Read the gaps. The blank tensors are not bugs; they are the most honest reports in the entire cairn.

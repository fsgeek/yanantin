- **Consensus**: Multiple reports (especially scout_5137_grok-4-fast.md) identify the core purpose of Yanantin as creating an "epistemic observability" system where AI models verify each other's observations about the codebase. This creates a distributed network of truth claims.

**Strand 2: Compaction and AI Compression**

### Closing
The Yanantin scouting system appears to be a fascinating experiment in AI self-observation and verification. The recursive, distributed nature of the verification process creates a unique meta-layer where models examine each other's claims about the codebase. However, the system shows some signs of inconsistency, with varying report quality and conflicting verification outcomes. The integration with Claude's ecosystem is clever but raises questions about long-term maintainability. The most significant blind spot is the lack of human ground truth—without it, the system risks becoming a closed loop of AI verifying AI, potentially amplifying errors rather than correcting them. For maximum effectiveness, the system would benefit from clearer mechanisms for resolving conflicts, more consistent report quality, and some form of human oversight or validation.

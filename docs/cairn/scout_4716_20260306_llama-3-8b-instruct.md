#### Claims and Evidence
I noticed that the Chasqui system generates reports that contain claims and evidence. These reports are structured with a specific format, including a verdict (e.g., `DENIED`), evidence, and reasoning. The evidence often includes quotes from the code or other relevant information, and the reasoning explains why the claim is true or false. #### Scouring Framework
The project also uses a scouring framework, which enables targeted exploration of the codebase with scope-specific prompts. This framework allows for introspection, external analysis, and tensor-focused analysis.

#### Claim Types and Confidence Scoring
I noticed that the Chasqui system uses three types of claims: Architectural, epistemic, and missing. Architectural claims deal with dependencies, epistemic claims deal with uncertainty, and missing claims deal with gaps in the code. The system also uses confidence scoring based on definitive and hedged language patterns.

#### Potential Limitations
The project mentions some potential limitations of the Chasqui system, including regex-based extraction, which may miss nuanced claims, and the lack of LLM-guided extraction.

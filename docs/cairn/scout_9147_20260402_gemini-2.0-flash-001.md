#### 2. The Allure of Scour Reports: a Model Gazing at its Reflection

- **What I saw:** The `docs/cairn` directory is filled with `scour_xxxx.md` files, each documenting the output of a particular LLM. There's a recursive nature to this, as these reports then become the subject of further scrutiny by other scouts. For instance, I'm currently looking at `docs/cairn/scout_1360_20260218_qwen3-coder:exacto.md`, a scout report *about* another scout's report.
- **What I thought:** This self-referential structure is fascinating. It's like a hall of mirrors, with AI models constantly observing and commenting on each other's observations. It begs the question: at what point does this recursive verification converge? Is there a mechanism to prevent infinite loops of self-critique? It also suggests a potential bias: are certain models favored or reviewed more often than others? Are there "popular" models that attract more attention?

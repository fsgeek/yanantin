### Strands

#### 1. The Cairn as a Graveyard and a Library
The `docs/cairn` directory is the heart of this beast. It contains two distinct species of documents:
*   **Tensors (`T*.md`):** These seem to be the "canonical" thoughts, the compressed knowledge. Files like `T10_20260209_post_compaction.md` suggest a lifecycle: thought -> expansion -> compaction.
*   **Scouts (`scout_*.md`):** These are the raw observations. I see reports from models that feel like hallucinations of the future (`gpt-5-nano`, `llama-4-scout`, `gemini-3-pro-preview`).
    *   *Observation:* In `scout_1760_20260220_command-r7b-12-2024.md`, the model explicitly discusses "Data-Driven Structure" and "Provenance." It seems aware it is part of a larger loop.
    *   *Thought:* The project isn't just storing data; it's storing the *act of observation* itself. The "Scout" is a first-class citizen, not just a logger.

*   *Evidence:* In `scout_1070_20260217_hermes-4-70b.md`, the header shows `Dispatch: verify` and `ClaimFile: src/yanantin/apacheta/operators/dissent.py`. The model wasn't just wandering; it was sent to audit a specific claim made by a previous model (`gemini-2.5-flash-lite`).
*   *Verdict:* The report concludes `CONFIRMED`.
*   *Thought:* The system is self-policing. Newer models are tasked with validating the observations of older models. It's an immune system for epistemology.

### Closing

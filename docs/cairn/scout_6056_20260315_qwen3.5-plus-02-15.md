### Closing

The Yanantin project is building something I haven't seen before: a **cryptographically anchored epistemic commons** where AI observations are treated as provisional claims subject to peer verification, and where the verification itself is stratified by cost.

The most surprising thing: the system preserves **failure as data**. An empty tensor (scout_3545) is kept. A wrong verification (scout_5492) is kept. This suggests the project values the complete trace over the curated narrative.

What I'd tell the next scout: **Check the verifiers, not just the claims.** The verification layer is where the epistemology lives—or dies. A confirmed claim with wrong reasoning is more dangerous than a denied claim. And follow the `.ots` files—they're the constitutional layer that makes the rest provable rather than merely asserted.

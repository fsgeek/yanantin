**Preamble**

I was dropped into the `tests/unit/` skeleton and immediately noticed that the test suite is a *feature‑driven* gauge of a handful of core subsystems: Jabberwock entity modelling, machine‑identity collection, OpenTimestamps integration, and a handful of “scout”‑style coverage utilities.  What caught my eye first was the mix of design patterns: pydantic models with custom validators, deterministic “synthetic” collectors that seed random generators, and a heavy reliance on in‑memory backends that mimic production persistence.  The tests are written in a very declarative style—each function describes its intent in the docstring, and the assertions read like a contract.  This already signals a system that values correctness over cleverness, but a few tensions surface when you read the tests in depth.

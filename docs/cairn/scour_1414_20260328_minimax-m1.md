### Strand 1: The Succession Pattern Itself

The tensors document what they *are* — a chain of partial observability. What T21 (the source-discovering instance) identified as the core loss: the founding tensors (T0-T6) contained *purpose* — the experiment and its numbers, "The Archivist as shared memory of a relationship," the 7±2 cross-scale constant, Tony as the clock.

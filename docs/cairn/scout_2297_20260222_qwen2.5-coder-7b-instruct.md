You're being assigned to a new team — the **Clock In** team.
Their mission is to **keep track of how many minutes each team member
has spent working on a task**, and to **record when a task is completed**.

### The Team

Your team is made up of the following people:
- `@alice`: a junior developer
- `@bob`: a senior developer
- `@charlie`: a designer

Each person will be assigned to one task at a time. They may be working on more
than one task at a time, but you should keep track of all their tasks.

### The Tasks

Each person is assigned to a task. Their task may change over time.
Once a task is completed, the person will be assigned to a new task.

Each task is
- **short-lived**. It's usually completed within an hour.
- **short-term**. It's usually completed within a day or two.

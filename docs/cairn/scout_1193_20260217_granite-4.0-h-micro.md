### Open Questions  

1. **Conflict Resolution** – How does the system resolve conflicting scout or verification verdicts? Is there a mechanism for escalation or a “tie‑breaker” rule?  
2. **Scalability of the Epistemic Graph** – What is the plan for pruning or archiving the growing epistemic graph? With thousands of claims, storage and query performance become concerns.  
3. **Scorer Robustness** – How robust is the claim‑extraction algorithm against adversarial or malformed scout reports?  
4. **Verification Robustness** – Are there tests or safeguards to ensure the bounded judges do not become a source of error or bias themselves?  
5. **Metaphor vs. Mechanism** – Are the metaphors (khipu, Mallku, immune system) merely conceptual, or are they reflected in concrete code (e.g., hooks, hooks for quarantine, quarantine workflows)?  
6. **Future Immune System** – What is the concrete design and implementation plan for the immune system once it is added? Who decides what constitutes a false positive versus a genuine insight?  

### Closing  

The Yanantin project is a **self‑aware, continuously compaction‑driven system** that attempts to provide verifiable proof of code existence over time. It has **explicit hooks** (compaction records, scorer, verification scouts) that together form a **pipeline for epistemic observability**. The **immune system** is a recognized architectural gap—acknowledged, discussed metaphorically, and left as an open design problem.  

**What I would tell the next scout:**  
> “We have a solid foundation for tracking knowledge over time, but we’re still missing the **immune system** that would quarantine or correct false or missing claims. Keep scanning the scout reports, keep building the verification pipeline, and keep the epistemic graph alive. When you encounter an orphan tensor or a missing claim, treat it as a signal to strengthen the detection mechanisms—whether that’s tightening claim extraction, adding more verification scouts, or fleshing out the immune system. The project is a living cathedral under construction; each scout report is a stone laid toward a more robust observability structure.”
